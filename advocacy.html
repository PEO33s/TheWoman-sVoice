Campaign write-up · Mensahe ng kampanya

A voice against violence

English

Behind every closed door is a story that deserves to be heard. This campaign calls attention to domestic violence against women, challenges the silence and stigma that surround it, and asks communities to respond with compassion. Every woman deserves to live safely, freely, and with dignity. Listen to survivors, believe them, and help connect them with trusted support.

Filipino

Sa likod ng bawat saradong pinto ay may kuwentong nararapat mapakinggan. Binibigyang-pansin ng kampanyang ito ang karahasan sa tahanan laban sa kababaihan at hinahamon ang pananahimik at stigma na bumabalot dito. Nararapat mabuhay ang bawat babae nang ligtas, malaya, at may dignidad. Pakinggan at suportahan ang mga nakararanas nito, at tulungan silang makalapit sa mapagkakatiwalaang suporta.
